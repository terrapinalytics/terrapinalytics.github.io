Data & method

247Sports Composite, historical listings collected October 28, 2026. National-rank filters include ties. 4★+ uses displayed Composite stars.

Approximate listed high-school cities, not verified hometowns. Academy concentrations count where the school is located. Missing coordinates are excluded from heat and circle counts.

Heat shows smoothed annual recruits per 10,000 square miles. Gaussian smoothing uses the selected radius as its standard deviation, cut off at three times that radius. The color scale defaults to the hottest visible cell; lock it to compare regions. Density extends across coasts and borders.

Circles use great-circle miles, not driving distance or exclusive recruiting territory. School membership is fixed to the dataset’s 67 power programs plus Notre Dame; campuses are approximate. Overlapping circles count a recruit for each school.

Top-1000 coverage varies by class; these are observed records, not an assertion that every class has exactly 1,000 players. The 2026 class reflects the source snapshot. Data describe displayed destinations, not independently verified enrollment.
