# Recruiting geography

Source: existing 247Sports Composite historical high-school ranking listings, collected October 28, 2026. Classes 2010–2026; observed national ranks through 1,000 including ties. US-only records: 16,760; geocoded: 16,536. US bluechips: 6,710; mapped: 6,619. Four/five stars use displayed Composite stars. The top-100/300/500/1000 filters use national rank, independent of star threshold. Each player-class record counts once.

Coordinates are approximate listed high-school cities, not verified hometowns or school building addresses. Source profiles and listed destinations are supplied for inspection. Displayed commitments are not independently verified enrollment. Academy/boarding-school clusters contribute fully. The data are the existing snapshot, not a fresh scrape.

Heat: Gaussian density with selectable 15/25/50-mile standard deviation and three-sigma cutoff. Samples use Web Mercator coordinates converted to local miles at each recruit latitude. This is an approximate density rendering, not an equal-area statistical surface, especially near the poles or over broad latitude spans. At national zoom the pixel grid can miss narrow peaks; zoom for detailed comparisons. Counts and great-circle circle statistics do not depend on heat pixels. Colors represent annual-average recruits per 10,000 square miles, divided by all selected classes including years with zero matches. Low densities use a power color scale (gamma 0.6). Dynamic scaling follows the maximum visible grid cell; lock the current scale before comparing regions. Filters reset scale lock so different populations are not silently compared on an obsolete scale. Density extends into water and across borders, with no boundary correction.

Campus overlay: fixed dataset membership of 67 power conference football programs plus Notre Dame, approximate campus points. Auto-display at zoom 5.5. Recruit dots appear at zoom 6.5. Circles are spherical great-circle radii; nearby competition and overlap remain visible. Distance statistics count all mapped filtered recruits inside a circle and their displayed commitments to that program. They do not divide recruits among overlapping schools. Missing-coordinate records are included in unfiltered coverage/export totals but excluded from map, view and distance statistics. If circle filtering is enabled, missing-coordinate records cannot be classified and are excluded.

State boundaries: PublicaMundi/MappingAPI us-states GeoJSON. City geocodes: kelvins/US-Cities-Database (MIT license). National rank and Composite definitions may change over time, and available top-1000 coverage varies by class. Current model outputs are not used in this map; capture rates are descriptive counts. No NIL, offers, scholarship capacity or position demand is inferred.
