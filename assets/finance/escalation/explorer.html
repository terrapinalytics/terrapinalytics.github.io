Sources and definitions

Dollar amounts are nominal or stated in FY2025 dollars using CPI-U. They show median reported spending per school or team. For scope and coverage, inspect a fiscal year using your pointer or arrow keys. Missing observations appear as gaps. Each series shows its available years in the picker.

Index and cumulative change rebase the published spending series to the chosen fiscal year. Annual growth uses each source series’ reported year-over-year change. The 3-year CAGR is the compound annual growth rate over three years. First year of each run uses a separate base for each uninterrupted span, so lines with different starting years show growth from different dates. The fixed-weight composite and CPI-U are index series, so they have no dollar values.

Earlier NCAA spending and football values use the original and backfilled filings. Sample counts may vary and annual growth uses schools present in both years. The comparable facilities, remaining-expense and composite indices begin in FY2015 because NCAA changed the facilities definition. The seven-school games and travel comparison starts in FY2024.

Spending indices reflect changes in the amount and composition of spending. Chart values describe spending escalation. NCAA budget categories and EADA sports use different cohorts and accounting definitions. Dollar medians and the median of school-level changes are distinct calculations.
