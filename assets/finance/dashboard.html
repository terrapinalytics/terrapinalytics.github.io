Read before comparing: NCAA/Knight-Newhouse figures and EADA figures differ in reporting perimeter and classification, including football spending, support, and revenue allocation. Never treat the two series as interchangeable or combine them into a continuous trend. The 2025-dollar option adjusts each fiscal year's amount using the calendar-year annual average U.S. CPI-U for the year the fiscal period ends. Multi-year averages use consecutive fiscal years ending in the selected year and require a usable value in every year. Trend points use the same trailing window. Peer lines give equal weight to schools with complete data for each point. Conference grouping refers to current affiliation, not conference membership in each historical fiscal year. NCAA observations are unavailable for many private institutions. Nine flagged FY2012 NCAA football spending zeros are excluded from comparisons. Historical source data were compiled in 2026; not every record was independently reconciled to its original line item. EADA football revenue and assigned football expenses may reflect allocation accounting, not commercial profit. NCAA figures draw on the Knight-Newhouse College Athletics Database, a joint project of the Knight Commission on Intercollegiate Athletics and Syracuse University’s S.I. Newhouse School of Public Communications, including some figures carried through secondary transcriptions. EADA is from the U.S. Department of Education. Group of Six coverage is limited to the 11 schools in the program rankings. Their NCAA line-item coverage varies by year. Reported institutional/government support and student fees are summed where both are available. Download nominal NCAA panel · Download nominal EADA panel.
