Read before comparing: NCAA/Knight-Newhouse figures and EADA figures differ in reporting perimeter and classification, including football spending, support, and revenue allocation. Never treat the two series as interchangeable or combine them into a continuous trend. This panel uses nominal dollars without inflation adjustment. Conference grouping refers to current affiliation, not conference membership in each historical fiscal year. NCAA observations are unavailable for many private institutions; a missing observation is never a reported zero. Historical source data were compiled in 2026; not every record was independently reconciled to its original line item.
