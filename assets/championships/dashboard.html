Method. A credit is one school–sport–title type record; shared titles and regular season/tournament double credits are retained. The optional weighted view uses tier weights (5, 3, 2, 1) and assigns each sport-season a fixed point budget, split 60/40 for regular-season/tournament titles where both occur, with shares divided among joint champions. Weighted points are assigned to source records before filtering, so filtering schools or types does not redistribute points.
