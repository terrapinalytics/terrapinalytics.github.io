Title types. Regular-season titles come from conference standings. Tournaments crown a winner through a bracket of team matchups, as in basketball. Championship events award a team title through a scored meet, as in swimming, or a single title game, as in football. A school can earn both a regular-season title and a postseason title in the same sport and season. Big Ten championship and tournament calendar.

Counting. Each co-champion normally gets one title credit. Turn on Split shared titles to divide one credit equally across all co-champions, including those hidden by filters. Tier-weighted points (5, 3, 2, 1 by tier) already split points among joint champions. Where a sport awards both regular-season and postseason titles, the regular season gets 60% of its point budget and the postseason gets 40%.
