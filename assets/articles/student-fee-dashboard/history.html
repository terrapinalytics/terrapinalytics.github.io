Coverage and notes

All 23 schools with positive amounts in the prior inventory are included. The panel starts in 2009–10 and runs through 2026–27. It contains 224 annual charge values across 414 school-years, including 32 pre-introduction zeros for the specific standalone Clemson and South Carolina charges. There are 336 comparable NCAA annual revenue observations, covering FY2010–FY2025 for 21 schools. Miami and Northwestern have billing evidence with no comparable NCAA financial figures in the panel.

  • Maryland went from $394 in 2010–11 to $399 in 2026–27. Georgia Tech went from $246 to $346 over the same span.
  • Clemson and South Carolina added $300 standalone annual charges in fall 2025. A pre-introduction zero means this separately named fee had yet to start. It does not establish an absence of other support.
  • Louisville lists $200 per semester in the current bursar schedule, correcting the earlier inventory’s $450 annual entry to $400.
  • Minnesota shows older stadium-only amounts where identifiable. In 2025–26, the $200 annual Gopher Athletics Facilities Fee joined the $12 annual stadium charge. The earlier rows marked stadium component only have a narrower scope.

Method and limits

Amounts are nominal dollars. Annual charges typically cover two fall/spring terms. Per-credit rates use a 30-credit illustration. A named fall-quarter-only fee counts once. A single historical bill or a reference with no billing frequency remains unannualized. The CSV preserves source URLs, posted rates, annualization factors and statuses. Displayed values are rounded to whole dollars. The year of the displayed Arizona State current schedule is not explicit and the row is marked accordingly.

Academic years align to the NCAA fiscal year ending in the following calendar year. Reported student-fee revenue is an aggregate allocation. Campuswide eligibility, exemptions, blended rates and fiscal accounting can change the relationship between this total and a published full-time undergraduate charge. The NCAA record does not supply a substitute per-student charge where a schedule is missing. Historical amounts are left blank where the billing basis or year is unresolved.
