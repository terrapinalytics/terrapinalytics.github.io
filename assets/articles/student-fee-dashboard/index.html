Student athletics costs

Compare mandatory athletics charges and standard student ticket prices across 68 schools.

Methodology

Costs reflect a typical full-time undergraduate over two regular terms. Credit-based illustrations use 15 credits per term unless the school uses a specific block or cap. Only the athletics-related portion of tuition and mandatory fees enters the calculation.

Published charges take precedence. When the athletics share is unpublished, an enrollment-based estimate divides reported athletics student-fee revenue by an enrollment proxy. A zero charge requires reported zero NCAA student-fee revenue and a fee schedule with no identified athletics allocation.

A zero ticket price indicates broad free access across regular-season home sports, subject to availability. Standard paid season options count even where students can use free claims. Guest tickets, postseason admission and extra donor benefits are outside the comparison. A subtotal includes only priced components. Displayed dollar amounts are rounded to the nearest dollar.
