How the rates are calculated

Team eFG% = (FGM + 0.5 × 3PM) / FGA. TO% = TO / estimated possessions, where possessions = FGA − ORB + TO + 0.475 × FTA. OR% = ORB / (ORB + opponent DRB). DR% = DRB / (DRB + opponent ORB). FT rate = FTA / FGA. Team rebound totals follow the ESPN box score. The turnover breakdown uses opponent steals and the remaining turnovers. Each percentage uses estimated offensive possessions as its denominator.

Player eFG% uses their own attempts. Player TO% uses TO / (FGA + 0.475 × FTA + TO), and player FT rate uses their own FTA / FGA. Player OR% and DR% divide the player’s rebounds by estimated opportunities while on the floor. Opportunities use player-credited rebounds on each end (own ORB + opponent DRB, or own DRB + opponent ORB), multiplied by five times the player’s minutes divided by total team player minutes. Blank rates indicate unavailable data or a zero denominator.

Colors compare with season rates pooled from available completed games before the displayed matchup. Shooting splits use accuracy for shading. Player totals compare with per-game averages across prior appearances. Lower turnover rates receive green shading. Rates with no season comparison are neutral. In the player box, OR% is above DR%. Fewer individual turnovers and fouls shade green.
