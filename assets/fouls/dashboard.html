Coverage, definitions, and limits

Team and referee views cover 2020–21 through 2025–26 and 2026–27 through November 17. The broad player view covers the current season. Historical player seasons currently contain Zach Edey’s Purdue profile only.

Team possession estimates average the two teams’ FGA − offensive rebounds + total turnovers + 0.475 × free-throw attempts. Player exposure uses the average of offensive and defensive possessions with the player in the source’s reconstructed on-court lineup. These lineups have not been independently video-verified.

Total team fouls come from box scores. Specific foul-type comparisons and foul-mix charts use only games where both teams’ play-by-play counts reconcile to their box totals under a documented counting convention. Missing and ambiguous types remain unresolved. The eligibility note reports the reduced sample when a type is selected.

Referee statistics describe the entire crew’s games. The source does not attribute individual whistles to an official. Rankings are raw descriptive rates, without opponent or crew adjustment. Foul margins also reflect team style, shot selection, and intentional fouling. Referee home edge excludes neutral-site games and is visitor fouls minus home fouls per 100 possessions.

Other non-shooting personal includes bonus and non-bonus calls. Flagrant and loose-ball categories are retained separately. Edey’s freshman administrative technical is outside his box personal-foul total, so his recorded committed-event total is one higher. Broad player totals count recorded committed events, including technicals.
