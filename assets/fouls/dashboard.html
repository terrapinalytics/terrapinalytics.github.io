Coverage, definitions, and limits

Team, referee, and player views cover 2018–19 through the current season. Current-season box scores and available referee crews run through November 22. Reconciled foul types and tracked player possessions currently run through November 17. Games from November 18 onward contribute box-score totals and available crew assignments.

Team possession estimates average the two teams’ FGA − offensive rebounds + total turnovers + 0.475 × free-throw attempts. Player exposure uses the average of offensive and defensive possessions with the player in the source’s reconstructed on-court lineup. These lineups have not been independently video-verified.

Total team fouls come from box scores. Team and crew foul-type comparisons and team game-log breakdowns use only games where both teams’ play-by-play counts reconcile to their box totals. Player mixes use recorded player events with tracked type detail. Missing and ambiguous types remain unresolved. The eligibility note reports the reduced sample when a type is selected.

Referee statistics describe the entire crew’s games. The source does not attribute individual whistles to an official. Rankings are raw descriptive rates, without opponent or crew adjustment. Foul margins also reflect team style, shot selection, and intentional fouling. Referee home edge excludes neutral-site games and is visitor fouls minus home fouls per 100 possessions.

Other non-shooting personal includes bonus and non-bonus calls. The profile mix and game log group loose-ball, flagrant, technical, and unresolved calls as Other. The leaderboard filter offers finer categories. Edey’s freshman administrative technical is outside his box personal-foul total, so his recorded committed-event total is one higher. Player totals count recorded committed events, including technicals. Historical drawn-foul types use a unique opposing foul at the same clock time when available. Other drawn-foul types remain unresolved.
